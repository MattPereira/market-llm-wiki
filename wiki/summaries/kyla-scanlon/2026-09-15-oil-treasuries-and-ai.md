# Oil, Treasuries, and AI

**Kyla Scanlon** · 2026-09-15 · 2:51 · [watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Vr085pEcro)

## The chain

Scanlon connects three stresses: expensive oil, Treasury yields above 5%, and an
AI industry consuming vast amounts of capital.

She says the Iran war had already constrained shipping through the Strait of
Hormuz. Saudi Arabia's roughly 7-million-barrel-per-day cross-country pipeline
provided a workaround until drones hit it last week. Its shutdown pushed oil
higher, raising shipping, food, and transportation costs and adding inflationary
pressure. Markets therefore expect the Fed may hike at Wednesday's meeting. The
10-year Treasury yield crossed 5% for the first time since 2023, increasing the
rate underlying mortgages, corporate borrowing, and financing for roughly $40
trillion of federal debt.

## AI adds demand for money

At the same time, AI labs, chipmakers, data-center builders, Big Tech, and the US
government all want financing from the same investors. Nvidia helps finance AI
deals, while Jensen Huang and President Trump publicly dismissed calls to slow
AI development; Trump favors little regulation. Because AI companies fear losing
if they do not build, they will borrow aggressively, forcing issuers to offer
higher yields.

Scanlon's conclusion: war-driven energy inflation and AI's capital demand both
push borrowing costs upward. Yet AI's ambition to become “the oil of the next 20
years” depends on the two inputs now disappearing—cheap power and cheap money.
