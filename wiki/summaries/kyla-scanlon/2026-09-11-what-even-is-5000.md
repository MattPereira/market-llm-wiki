# What even is $5,000

**Kyla Scanlon** · 2026-09-11 · 3:00 · [watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q_22U1wdbWY)

## The dividend does not fund itself

Scanlon tests Trump's proposal to give every adult US citizen a $5,000 “Trump
dividend” funded by tariffs. Paying roughly 240 million adults would cost about
$1.2 trillion. Yet customs receipts were negative in May, June, and July as the
government refunded IEEPA tariffs ruled unlawful by the Supreme Court. About
$100 billion has been returned, roughly half the amount owed, while the tariffs
still in force are projected to raise just $635 billion over 10 years.

## The bill becomes debt

The government would therefore borrow into debt above $40 trillion, a projected
$2.1 trillion deficit, and annual interest near $1 trillion. At then-current
Treasury yields—5.35% for the 30-year and nearly 5% for the 10-year—the new debt
could add roughly $60 billion in yearly interest. Oil above $100 amid the Iran
war also keeps inflation and rates under pressure.

Congress must authorize the payment. Neither the proposed $5,000 DOGE dividend
nor a later $2,000 tariff dividend was enacted. Scanlon's conclusion: the promise
repeats failed precedents and shifts today's payout into future fiscal costs.
