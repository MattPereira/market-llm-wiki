# Bonds, a Beacon of Truth in Our Dark World

**Kyla Scanlon** · 2026-09-24 · 2:30 · [watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MOebsrvXQDQ)

## AI enthusiasm, government uncertainty

Scanlon sees two competing forces: investors want exposure to America's AI boom,
but political and trade uncertainty makes them demand higher returns for lending
to its government. **Higher US yields transmit pressure globally**: other countries
must offer more to compete for capital seeking returns and safety.

## Diversifying safety

The Iran war adds energy uncertainty. America has traditionally been the refuge,
but Scanlon says doubts about the dollar and government encourage gold holdings.
She cites the Dutch central bank moving about **86 tons of gold from New York and
Ottawa to London between March and August**, seeking access during a crisis.
She reads this modest move as a diversification signal.

US–China talks could ease uncertainty if trade continues, or worsen it through
more tariffs and disputes over chips and AI. Scanlon expects the talks to cover
AI, tariffs, rare minerals, and the war in Iran.

## Bonds flag what stocks overlook

Despite stocks rising roughly 1% that day, Scanlon sees bonds signaling worry.
She argues equities' dependence on AI success encourages investors to overlook
structural risks in hopes the technology resolves them; bonds express skepticism.
