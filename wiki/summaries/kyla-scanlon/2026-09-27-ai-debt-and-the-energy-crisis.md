# AI Debt and the Energy Crisis

**Kyla Scanlon** · 2026-09-27 · 2:37 · [watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C2Z1h4SoOOc)

## Energy inflation raises borrowing costs

Scanlon links the Iran war's energy disruption to inflation and Fed tightening.
She says diesel reached **$6.53 per gallon on September 22**, its fourth record
that month. Because diesel transports goods, higher prices spread throughout
the economy. She reports the 10-year Treasury yield finished the week above
**5.16%**, raising mortgage and other borrowing costs.

Higher yields benefit investors seeking bond income, including retirees, while
hurting aspiring homeowners and people carrying debt. She sees this widening
the divide between asset owners and people dependent on wages.

## AI borrowing feeds the squeeze

Scanlon says the five biggest tech companies are spending roughly **$800 billion
on data centers and chips this year**, financing the buildout through bonds.
Competition with government borrowing for the same capital pushes financing
costs higher, including for AI projects themselves.

She cites Oracle's New Mexico OpenAI data center project as under strain,
reports record debt-insurance costs, and says Oracle sits one downgrade above
junk. Her conclusion: energy inflation, tightening, and AI borrowing reinforce
each other, squeezing AI debt.
