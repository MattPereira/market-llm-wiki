# Taiki: The Trading Chad

**Steady Lads Podcast** · 2026-09-11 · 59:05 · [watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LgzowlHh-NA)

## Thesis

The panel reads the market as constructive but highly selective: Taiki is
max-long ZEC, Jordy prefers Bitcoin as a dollar-debasement trade, and the favored
alts are venues or narrative assets with identifiable catalysts. Robinhood's
stock-paired memecoins show returning risk appetite, not new fundamental value.

## Positioning and calls

- **Taiki is max-long ZEC.** His public Variational P&L is tied almost entirely
  to Zcash. He ranks the conviction alongside his former Fartcoin long and above
  his ETH short, while acknowledging that his highest-conviction trades often
  end in large round trips.
- **Exit framework:** start taking profit around **3–4% of Bitcoin's market cap**;
  Dim offers **5%** as the moon-math target. Taiki also watches the shielded-supply
  trend and his own euphoria. Jordy has already trimmed after the move through
  $1,000 and expects deep, recurring drawdowns.
- **Bitcoin over broad alts.** Jordy buys around $77K but is not certain the bear
  market is over; a return to the high $50Ks remains plausible. He expects BTC to
  attract macro flows if a stalled equity market sends investors elsewhere and
  dollar debasement stays the dominant story.
- **Worldcoin as an OpenAI sympathy trade.** The case is OpenAI's improving
  secondary-market demand, Sam Altman's association with both projects, and a
  hoped-for Q4 presentation—not demonstrated token value capture.
- **Lighter over HYPE at current valuations.** The panel sees better monetization
  and regulatory positioning in Lighter. HYPE is considered expensive in the
  $80s, with staking issuance allegedly exceeding buybacks and large recurring
  unlocks; Taiki says Lighter should be worth at least 10% of HYPE.
- **BNB is Jordy's laggard pick:** Binance has built liquid tokenized-stock spot
  and perp markets, could regain a US narrative after a CZ pardon, and trades
  near a chart level he treats as a ~$600 floor; he targets roughly $900. The
  panel concedes BNB's burns are reserve-supply reductions, not true revenue-funded
  buybacks, so exchange success need not accrue to the token.

## Zcash case—and its limits

- Bull case: privacy as a Bitcoin upgrade, more shielded supply as usage evidence,
  proactive work on quantum risk, concentrated holders perceived as less likely
  to sell, and an insurance role if Bitcoin develops a technical problem.
- Trading case: without cash-flow valuation anchors, ZEC is driven by psychology,
  relative-market-cap targets, and reflexivity, making overshoots likely both up
  and down.
- The hosts do not establish that shielded-supply growth equals distinct users,
  quantify quantum risk, or show that ZEC would retain value during a Bitcoin
  failure. Their price targets are analogies, not valuation models.

## Macro regime

The panel expects a rate hike after a hot-enough CPI print, potentially before the
midterms. Ordinarily that raises the opportunity cost of Bitcoin and gold, but
Jordy argues Treasury support for long-duration debt means continued money creation
and a weaker dollar regardless. He reads risk assets rising on the inflation print
as evidence that markets share that view. The group remains split: Dim sees a
renewed bull market, while Justin says Bitcoin has not endured enough washout to
rule out new lows.

## Tokenized stocks and trading culture

- Robinhood stock/memecoin pairs are a cultural bridge from GME-style retail
  trading into crypto. Pairing a meme with an equity may create attention, but it
  does not give the meme a claim on the stock or make it intrinsically valuable.
- Hunter Biden's **LAPTOP** launch briefly printed a nonsensical ~$316B valuation
  after only ~$5K of launch liquidity met sniping bots, then collapsed. The panel
  treats it as operational incompetence rather than a fundamentally different
  category from other celebrity tokens—and criticizes selective moral outrage
  from accounts that routinely promote memecoins.
- PONZI/Pawns' revenue spike, Loracle's public short, rapid leaderboard reversals,
  and short-lived Solana memes all illustrate the same dynamic: attention creates
  tradable volatility, but public P&L and early revenue invite reflexive crowding
  and round trips.
- Dim's broader thesis: tokenized US shares export dollar-denominated assets to
  savers who lack local brokerage access. That could make Binance, Robinhood, and
  on-chain perp venues strategically important distributors of US capital-market
  exposure.

## Caveats

- Taiki trades on and promotes Variational, including an unsubstantiated claim
  that its swaps deliver roughly 10× better execution than competing TradFi perps.
- Jordy discloses positions in ZEC, BNB, and Prenetics and discusses launching a
  Prenetics-paired memecoin. Taiki is an angel investor in Lighter. Their calls
  are heavily aligned with their books.
- The proposed Binance US return, IPO, CZ pardon effects, Worldcoin catalyst, BNB
  chart floor, and ZEC/BTC ratios are speculative scenarios presented without
  supporting data.
